How we work with you

  1. 1

    Listen

    We start with what the money is for: payments you must make, goals you want to reach and losses you cannot accept.

  2. 2

    Agree a mandate

    We write down the target return, the risk limits and what we will never buy. You sign it before we invest a dollar.

  3. 3

    Invest gradually

    We build positions over weeks or months, which avoids committing everything at a single price.

  4. 4

    Report plainly

    Each quarter you get a four-page report: what we did, what it cost, what went wrong and what we will change.
